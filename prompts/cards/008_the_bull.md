# 008 - The Bull
*Horns Toward Heaven*

- **Rarity:** epic  |  **Category:** force  |  **Type:** Season  |  **Alignment:** Greed
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A phenomenon rendered as weather, tide, fire or gravity — not a character.
The subject is an event happening to a landscape or an architecture: a surge,
a collapse, a freezing, a burning, a turning wheel.

If a figure appears at all it is small, anonymous, and present only for scale.
Motion is carried by light, particulate and atmosphere: sparks, ash, spray,
dust, sheets of rain, shockwaves through haze. Strong directional energy
through the frame, resolving into darkness at the base.

## Scene beats

- Momentum: Makes bad decisions look like early ones.
- Recruitment: Arrives with strangers who have never seen a chart fall.
- Amnesia: Forgets every winter in roughly eleven weeks.

## Frame and finish

High grandeur, one step below the chase cards.

Painterly oil rendering on a near-black ground, dramatic single key light with
strong rim separation, volumetric haze. Oxidised-gold ornament present but
more restrained than mythic: engraved borders implied inside the scene,
bronze hardware, wax and parchment accents. Ember warmth in the highlights,
cool contrast confined to a narrow rim.

A subtle iridescent sheen may catch across broad surfaces — the whole card
takes a holo overlay in production, so leave large mid-tone areas that can
carry it without turning muddy.

Composition: centred and iconic, subject in the upper two thirds, base third
quiet and low-contrast. Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: full holo overlay, gold foil title and icons.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Bull is not a price but a mood that spends money. It rewards the reckless first, the patient second, and the late never. Its greatest trick is convincing an entire cohort that this is simply how markets behave now.
