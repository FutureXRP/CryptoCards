# 068 — The MEV Bot  (epic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: lives in the half-second; reads intent; the toll of being visible.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A sleek mantis-like automaton of black steel and bronze poised on a single glowing golden thread within a vast dark web of such threads, its narrow head lit by two cold blue eyes, forelimbs raised mid-strike. Motion-blur ghosts of its own body trail behind it. One large engraved bronze ring frames the web's centre. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred iconic frontal composition, subject occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
