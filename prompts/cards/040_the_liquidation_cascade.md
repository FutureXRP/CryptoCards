# 040 — The Liquidation Cascade  (epic / force)

Scene beats from lore: one forced sale prices the next; nobody selling by choice; arithmetic all the way down.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A curved line of colossal bronze dominoes toppling through a dark pillared hall, the nearest slab caught mid-fall, ember dust bursting from each impact. Tiny robed figures flee between the pillars. The farthest dominoes vanish into darkness, still standing, waiting. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred composition with the event occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
