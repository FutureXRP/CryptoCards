# 071 — The Smart Contract  (epic / relic)

Scene beats from lore: no judge, no jury, only execution; pays saint and thief alike; exactly what was written.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A massive upright slab door of dark bronze filling an alcove, its face embedded with interlocking plain gears and one keyhole of white light at the centre, caught at the instant of turning. Thin lines of molten gold trace the seams between its plates. The floor before it is bare polished stone. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred still-life composition, the object occupying the middle of the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
