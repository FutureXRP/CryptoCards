# 064 - The Rugged Chat
*Ten Thousand Silent Members*

- **Rarity:** uncommon  |  **Category:** realm  |  **Type:** Wake  |  **Alignment:** Fear
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A place, rendered as architecture or landscape with mythic scale. Wide
establishing view compressed into a portrait frame: vaults, halls, forests,
substations, cathedral interiors of machinery, drowned plazas.

Perspective leads the eye to a single luminous focal point in the upper half.
Human figures, if any, are tiny and anonymous. Atmosphere does the heavy work
— haze, shafts of light, distance fog. The foreground base is dark and
uncluttered.

## Scene beats

- Wake: A room that outlived the thing it gathered for.
- Pinned Lie: The last announcement is still at the top.
- Loyalty: Somebody still checks in every year.

## Frame and finish

Grounded and workmanlike, still unmistakably part of the set.

Painterly oil rendering, black and bronze palette with more grey and less
gold. Single key light, modest rim, minimal haze. Ornament limited to one or
two silver or pewter accents — the title prints in silver foil, so favour
cooler metal in the scene than the higher tiers.

Composition: centred, subject in the upper two thirds, base third quiet.
Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: silver foil title and a standard holo border.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The channel survives the project by years, a room full of people who never left. The pinned message still describes a launch that happened to someone else. Occasionally a newcomer asks whether the team is active, and the silence answers.

## Negative prompt

text, letters, words, numerals, watermark, signature, logo, brand marks, ticker symbols, real people, celebrity likeness, existing IP characters, photorealistic faces, low contrast, flat lighting, cartoon, meme style, extra fingers.
