# 029 — The Seed Phrase  (epic / relic)

Scene beats from lore: twelve words, everything or nothing; stamped in steel; memory sized.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A small steel tablet resting on dark velvet atop a stone plinth, its face bearing twelve small blank recessed cells in three neat columns, each empty cell glowing faintly ember from within. A ring of unmarked wax seals surrounds it. Behind the plinth leans one large engraved bronze ring, plain and worn. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred still-life composition, the object occupying the middle of the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
