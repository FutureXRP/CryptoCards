# 073 — Proof of Work  (epic / force)

Scene beats from lore: energy into truth; outspend history to rewrite it; the honest furnace.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A colossal furnace in a dark industrial hall, its mouth a wall of ember light, molten gold pouring into a rough block mould on an anvil of black iron. A lone smith stands silhouetted against the glare, small as a child before it. One great engraved bronze cog, taller than the smith, leans against the furnace wall. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred composition with the event occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
