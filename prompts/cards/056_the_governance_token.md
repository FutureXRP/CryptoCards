# 056 - The Governance Token
*Voice, Weighted by Wallet*

- **Rarity:** uncommon  |  **Category:** token  |  **Type:** Charter  |  **Alignment:** Order
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A minted form — coin, ingot, seal, medallion, shard — treated as an idol
rather than as currency. Centred, floating or held in an armature, lit from
one side with a hard key and a cold rim.

The face of the coin carries an abstract sigil, an engraved figure, or a
geometric device — never letters, never numerals, never a real token logo.
Surface tells the story: mirror-polished, corroded, cracked, molten,
counterfeit-thin, impossibly heavy. Base of the frame quiet and dark.

## Scene beats

- Ceremony: The vote is real; the result was arithmetic.
- Apathy: Almost nobody votes until the treasury is threatened.
- Delegation: Power collects quietly with a few reliable names.

## Frame and finish

Grounded and workmanlike, still unmistakably part of the set.

Painterly oil rendering, black and bronze palette with more grey and less
gold. Single key light, modest rim, minimal haze. Ornament limited to one or
two silver or pewter accents — the title prints in silver foil, so favour
cooler metal in the scene than the higher tiers.

Composition: centred, subject in the upper two thirds, base third quiet.
Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: silver foil title and a standard holo border.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Governance Token promises a parliament and usually delivers a shareholder meeting with better graphics. Participation is low, delegation is fashionable, and the largest holder is patient. When the vote matters, the outcome arrives from a single address at the last hour.

## Negative prompt

text, letters, words, numerals, watermark, signature, logo, brand marks, ticker symbols, real people, celebrity likeness, existing IP characters, photorealistic faces, low contrast, flat lighting, cartoon, meme style, extra fingers.
