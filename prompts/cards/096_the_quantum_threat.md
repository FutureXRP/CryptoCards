# 096 — The Quantum Threat  (epic / force)

Scene beats from lore: a machine learning to divide the indivisible; every signature a suggestion; the polite mortal race.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A chandelier-like machine of concentric gold rings and cold blue filaments suspended in a black laboratory void, hanging like an inverted cathedral above a floor covered with thousands of tiny bronze padlocks, the nearest of which are quietly crumbling to dust. Faint helium mist pools below. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred composition with the event occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
