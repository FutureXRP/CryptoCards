# 061 - The Multisig
*No One Hand Alone*

- **Rarity:** uncommon  |  **Category:** relic  |  **Type:** Covenant  |  **Alignment:** Order
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A single object presented as a museum artefact: centred, lit like a treasure,
floating or resting on dark stone, velvet or a worn plinth. Shallow depth of
field, hard key light, deep shadow behind.

The object is archaic-technological — engraved bronze, blackened steel,
lacquered wood, cut crystal, wax and vellum. It should look older than the
thing it represents. Fine detail concentrated in the upper two thirds, base
falling into shadow. Absolutely no lettering engraved anywhere on it.

## Scene beats

- Quorum: Power divided so that no single key is fatal.
- Friction: Every payment becomes a small committee meeting.
- Resilience: Survives the loss of any one participant.

## Frame and finish

Grounded and workmanlike, still unmistakably part of the set.

Painterly oil rendering, black and bronze palette with more grey and less
gold. Single key light, modest rim, minimal haze. Ornament limited to one or
two silver or pewter accents — the title prints in silver foil, so favour
cooler metal in the scene than the higher tiers.

Composition: centred, subject in the upper two thirds, base third quiet.
Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: silver foil title and a standard holo border.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Multisig replaces trust in a person with trust in a quorum, which is a genuine improvement and a genuine burden. Every emergency now requires a group chat and three time zones. Treasuries survive on it, and occasionally freeze under it.
