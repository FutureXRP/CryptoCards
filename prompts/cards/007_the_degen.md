# 007 — The Degen  (epic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: maximum size every time; the chart is a coin flip; would not change a trade.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A gaunt hooded gambler frozen mid-throw at a black stone table, dozens of plain bronze coins suspended in the air above his open hand, his eyes lit ember orange by their glow. Behind him leans one massive engraved bronze wheel, taller than a man, its rim worn from spinning. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred iconic frontal composition, subject occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
