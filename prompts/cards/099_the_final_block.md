# 099 — The Final Block  (legendary / force)

Scene beats from lore: scheduled past 2140; no living witness; the mint closes forever.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. At the far end of a mile-long bridge of pale light, a single dark monolithic block waits on a horizon of stars — the last block, unlit, unclaimed. The bridge is built of countless faintly glowing slabs receding toward it in perfect sequence, the near slabs warm gold, the far ones cooling to bone white. Tiny robed pilgrim figures stand at the bridge's near end, dwarfed, unable to follow. A thin gold ring hangs in the sky above the distant block like an eclipse. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain, gilt flaking at the extreme edges. Centred composition with the event occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The outermost edges of the image are dark, uniform and undisturbed. Painterly, not photorealistic, not 3D render. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no signature, no watermark, no logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
