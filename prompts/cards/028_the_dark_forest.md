# 028 - The Dark Forest
*Every Move Is Watched*

- **Rarity:** rare  |  **Category:** realm  |  **Type:** Wilds  |  **Alignment:** Fear
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A place, rendered as architecture or landscape with mythic scale. Wide
establishing view compressed into a portrait frame: vaults, halls, forests,
substations, cathedral interiors of machinery, drowned plazas.

Perspective leads the eye to a single luminous focal point in the upper half.
Human figures, if any, are tiny and anonymous. Atmosphere does the heavy work
— haze, shafts of light, distance fog. The foreground base is dark and
uncluttered.

## Scene beats

- Predation: Profitable and public cannot both be true for long.
- Automation: The hunters are scripts, not villains.
- Concealment: Safety costs a fee and a private path.

## Frame and finish

Strong, dignified, less ceremonial than the upper tiers.

Painterly oil rendering, black and bronze palette, one dramatic key light and
a soft rim. Ornament is architectural rather than decorative — the gold shows
up as hardware, engraving on objects in the scene, a seal or a fitting, not as
a border. Haze is thinner; the scene is more legible and less theatrical.

Composition: centred, subject in the upper two thirds, base third quiet.
Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: gold foil title and a foil rarity crest.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Dark Forest is not malicious, only efficient, which feels identical from the inside. Every broadcast intention is prey, and the predators are code that never sleeps or gloats. Survival here means moving privately, quickly, and without telling anyone why.

text, letters, words, numerals, watermark, signature, logo, brand marks, ticker symbols, real people, celebrity likeness, existing IP characters, photorealistic faces, low contrast, flat lighting, cartoon, meme style, extra fingers.
