# 026 — The Cypherpunk  (epic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: privacy taken in code; decades of night shifts; the scaffolding of everything.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A hooded figure at a candlelit desk crowded with brass machines, coiled punched tape and wax-sealed envelopes, the face hidden behind a smooth mirrored mask reflecting cold blue light. Behind the desk rises one large engraved bronze panel of plain circuit-like geometric linework. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred iconic frontal composition, subject occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
