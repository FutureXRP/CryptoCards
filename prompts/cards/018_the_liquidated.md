# 018 — The Liquidated  (epic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: calculated everything but the wick; gone between two prints; the screenshot survives.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A hollow, ash-grey trader slumped in a chair before a dead black slab of stone, papers burnt to curls around his feet. From the darkness above, one colossal candle of black glass descends like a blade, its lower wick glowing ember orange, its tip almost touching his brow. One engraved bronze column stands toppled beside the chair. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred iconic frontal composition, subject occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
