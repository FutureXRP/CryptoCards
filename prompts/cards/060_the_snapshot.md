# 060 - The Snapshot
*The Hour That Counted*

- **Rarity:** uncommon  |  **Category:** force  |  **Type:** Instant  |  **Alignment:** Order
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A phenomenon rendered as weather, tide, fire or gravity — not a character.
The subject is an event happening to a landscape or an architecture: a surge,
a collapse, a freezing, a burning, a turning wheel.

If a figure appears at all it is small, anonymous, and present only for scale.
Motion is carried by light, particulate and atmosphere: sparks, ash, spray,
dust, sheets of rain, shockwaves through haze. Strong directional energy
through the frame, resolving into darkness at the base.

## Scene beats

- Instant: One block decides who counts.
- Secrecy: Kept quiet so behaviour stays honest.
- Arbitrary: The threshold is a number somebody picked.

## Frame and finish

Grounded and workmanlike, still unmistakably part of the set.

Painterly oil rendering, black and bronze palette with more grey and less
gold. Single key light, modest rim, minimal haze. Ornament limited to one or
two silver or pewter accents — the title prints in silver foil, so favour
cooler metal in the scene than the higher tiers.

Composition: centred, subject in the upper two thirds, base third quiet.
Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: silver foil title and a standard holo border.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Snapshot turns a random block into the only moment that ever mattered. Years of participation are graded by a single frame, chosen quietly and revealed later. Everyone who consolidated wallets that morning learns something about timing.
