# 051 — The Genesis Block  (legendary / relic)

Scene beats from lore: where it all began; the first stone; the inscription that started everything.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A single rough-hewn cube of black stone resting on a low altar of packed earth, the first stone of an unbuilt cathedral whose ghostly gold scaffolding rises faintly into the darkness above it. One polished face of the cube glows with warm ember light from within, as if freshly quarried from a furnace. Candle stubs and drifted ash surround the altar; a vast engraved gold rosette looms half-lit behind. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain, gilt flaking at the extreme edges. Centred still-life composition, the object occupying the middle of the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The outermost edges of the image are dark, uniform and undisturbed. Painterly, not photorealistic, not 3D render. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no signature, no watermark, no logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
