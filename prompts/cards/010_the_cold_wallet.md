# 010 - The Cold Wallet
*Silence That Holds*

- **Rarity:** epic  |  **Category:** relic  |  **Type:** Reliquary  |  **Alignment:** Order
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A single object presented as a museum artefact: centred, lit like a treasure,
floating or resting on dark stone, velvet or a worn plinth. Shallow depth of
field, hard key light, deep shadow behind.

The object is archaic-technological — engraved bronze, blackened steel,
lacquered wood, cut crystal, wax and vellum. It should look older than the
thing it represents. Fine detail concentrated in the upper two thirds, base
falling into shadow. Absolutely no lettering engraved anywhere on it.

## Scene beats

- Distance: Keeps the keys where the internet cannot follow.
- Ritual: Every movement requires deliberate, physical intent.
- Patience: Rewards the owner who does nothing for years.

## Frame and finish

High grandeur, one step below the chase cards.

Painterly oil rendering on a near-black ground, dramatic single key light with
strong rim separation, volumetric haze. Oxidised-gold ornament present but
more restrained than mythic: engraved borders implied inside the scene,
bronze hardware, wax and parchment accents. Ember warmth in the highlights,
cool contrast confined to a narrow rim.

A subtle iridescent sheen may catch across broad surfaces — the whole card
takes a holo overlay in production, so leave large mid-tone areas that can
carry it without turning muddy.

Composition: centred and iconic, subject in the upper two thirds, base third
quiet and low-contrast. Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: full holo overlay, gold foil title and icons.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Cold Wallet is a promise kept by refusing to be convenient. It survives every clever attack by declining to be present for any of them. Its only true enemy is the owner's memory, which is not covered by the warranty.

## Negative prompt

text, letters, words, numerals, watermark, signature, logo, brand marks, ticker symbols, real people, celebrity likeness, existing IP characters, photorealistic faces, low contrast, flat lighting, cartoon, meme style, extra fingers.
