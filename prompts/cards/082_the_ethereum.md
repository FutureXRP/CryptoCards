# 082 — The Ethereum  (legendary / token)

Scene beats from lore: the world computer; a city grows beneath it; everything built on its land.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A great faceted octahedron of dark polished metal suspended in the air above an altar, turning slowly, its lower point aimed at a miniature city of thousands of tiny warm-lit windows growing up toward it like a garden reaching for light. Fine filaments of gold thread rise from the city's spires into the stone's facets. A monumental engraved gold arch frames the floating stone in the darkness. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain, gilt flaking at the extreme edges. Centred still-life composition, the token occupying the middle of the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The outermost edges of the image are dark, uniform and undisturbed. Painterly, not photorealistic, not 3D render. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no signature, no watermark, no logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
