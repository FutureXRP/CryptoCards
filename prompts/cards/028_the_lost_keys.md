# 028 — The Lost Keys  (legendary / relic)

Scene beats from lore: a fortune sealed forever; the twelve words forgotten; safe from everyone.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A small corroded iron strongbox bound in chains whose links have rusted into one mass, standing on a stone altar, its keyhole glowing with a faint ember light from deep inside. Above it, a ring of ancient bronze keys hangs from a fraying cord, forever out of reach. The whole altar is sheathed in a slab of dark glassy ice; a monumental engraved gold arch rises behind it in the gloom. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain, gilt flaking at the extreme edges. Centred still-life composition, the object occupying the middle of the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The outermost edges of the image are dark, uniform and undisturbed. Painterly, not photorealistic, not 3D render. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no signature, no watermark, no logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
