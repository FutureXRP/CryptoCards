# 098 — The Supercycle  (epic / force)

Scene beats from lore: summits declared over at the summit; sincere and early to every top; the mirage on real sand.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. An impossible mountain whose upper slopes curl away from gravity, its summit path spiralling upward into a sky of gold-lit cloud with no peak in sight. A procession of tiny climbers ascends carrying banners, the highest already walking on air. The mountain's base shimmers like heat-haze above dark sand. One large engraved bronze sun-disc hangs low behind the spiral. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred composition with the event occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
