# 008 — The Diamond Hands  (epic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: the grip crystallised; fingers that will not open; forged in crashes.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A kneeling armoured figure clutching a single plain bronze coin to the chest with hands of rough crystal that refract cold light, frost and hairline cracks running up both forearms. Behind the figure stands one great engraved bronze disc, plain-rimmed, catching the key light. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred iconic frontal composition, subject occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
