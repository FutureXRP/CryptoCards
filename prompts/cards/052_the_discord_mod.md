# 052 - The Discord Mod
*Unpaid, Unsleeping*

- **Rarity:** uncommon  |  **Category:** archetype  |  **Type:** Sentinel  |  **Alignment:** Order
- **Output:** 1024 x 1536 (2:3), no text of any kind, no logos, no real people.

## Subject

A single allegorical figure — a person as a force of character, not a
portrait of anyone real. Face partly shadowed or obscured by hood, mask,
helm, smoke or distance; features stylised toward myth rather than likeness.
Costume built from ledger-world materials: engraved plate, wax seals, chain,
coin-scale mail, parchment robes, bronze instruments of trade.

The figure should read instantly in silhouette. One gesture, one posture, one
telling prop. No crowd, no background characters, no recognisable modern
clothing or branding.

## Scene beats

- Vigilance: Reads every message so nobody else has to.
- Repetition: Answers the same question until language loses meaning.
- Exposure: First to be blamed, last to be credited.

## Frame and finish

Grounded and workmanlike, still unmistakably part of the set.

Painterly oil rendering, black and bronze palette with more grey and less
gold. Single key light, modest rim, minimal haze. Ornament limited to one or
two silver or pewter accents — the title prints in silver foil, so favour
cooler metal in the scene than the higher tiers.

Composition: centred, subject in the upper two thirds, base third quiet.
Outer ten pixels dark and uniform.

Finish this tier prints with: silver foil title and a standard holo border.

## Lore reference (for mood only - do not render as text)

> The Mod holds the last line between a community and the people impersonating it. The work is constant, thankless, and performed for a title in a coloured font. When the server survives an attack, nobody writes a post about who was awake.

## Negative prompt

text, letters, words, numerals, watermark, signature, logo, brand marks, ticker symbols, real people, celebrity likeness, existing IP characters, photorealistic faces, low contrast, flat lighting, cartoon, meme style, extra fingers.
