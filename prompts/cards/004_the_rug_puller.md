# 004 — The Rug Puller  (mythic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: the gilded hall; the floor was a curtain; already printing new invitations.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A slender showman in a gilded brocade coat striding away from the viewer through a decaying opulent hall, head turned just enough that the face remains lost in shadow, one hand sweeping up the edge of an enormous ornate carpet. Beneath the lifted carpet there is no floor at all, only a clean black void swallowing overturned chairs and scattered gilt. Guttering candelabra, peeling gold leaf, drifting dust in volumetric haze. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain, gilt flaking at the extreme edges. Centred iconic composition, the figure occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The outermost edges of the image are dark, uniform and undisturbed. Painterly, not photorealistic, not 3D render. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no signature, no watermark, no logos or brand marks anywhere in the image.
