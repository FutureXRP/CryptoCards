# 060 — The FTX Collapse  (epic / force)

Scene beats from lore: stadiums and presidents; the vault a promise; empty before they admitted it.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A grand marble and gold exchange temple caught mid-collapse, its dome cracking open, gold dust pouring from the ceiling like sand. Tiny figures stream down the steps carrying nothing. At the hall's heart a massive round vault door hangs open onto pure hollow darkness. One large engraved bronze pediment slides from the roofline. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred composition with the event occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
