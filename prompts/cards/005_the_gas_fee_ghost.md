# 005 — The Gas Fee Ghost  (epic / archetype)

Scene beats from lore: waits at the bridge; palm open; paid in calm and frenzy alike.

> Museum-quality oil painting on aged canvas, dark epic fantasy meets financial mythology. A towering spectral figure of translucent smoke and ember light standing on a narrow stone bridge over a black chasm, one skeletal bronze hand extended palm up, hood empty. A queue of tiny robed travellers waits at the bridge's near end, heads bowed. Beside the ghost stands one large engraved bronze toll-gate arch, its geometric ornament worn smooth. Palette strictly black, bronze, oxidised gold, ember orange and bone white, with one narrow cold blue rim light separating the silhouette from the dark and a single hard warm key light raking from the upper left. Painterly visible brushwork, impasto in the highlights, deep chiaroscuro falloff, aged varnish, fine canvas grain. Centred iconic frontal composition, subject occupying the upper two thirds of the frame, the lower third falling away into quiet near-black empty shadow with no detail. The painting runs fully to every edge of the image with no painted frame, no border, no matte, no vignette band — the outermost edges are dark, uniform and undisturbed open shadow. All carved and engraved surfaces are plain geometric ornament only. Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numerals, no runes, no glyphs, no inscriptions, no currency symbols, no signature, no watermark, no logos anywhere in the image.
