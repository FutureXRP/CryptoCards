# Legendary (10 cards — assignment lives in the manifest)

Ornament: near-Mythic. A full engraved gold structure frames the subject —
arch, rosette or monument — but without the Mythic tier's embossed sigil work.
Light: hard warm key plus one cold rim, and a faint halo behind the focal mass.
Presence: mythological. These are the set's icons (The Satoshi, The Halving,
The Bitcoin); paint them as monuments being witnessed, not portraits.
